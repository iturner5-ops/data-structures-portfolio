Charlotte Hornets Offseason Analysis

Research Questions: 

How has the Charlotte Hornets' 2026 offseason changed the team's short-term playoff potential?

Was trading LaMelo Ball the correct long-term decision for the Charlotte Hornets?

Problem Definition:

The Charlotte Hornets had one of there best years this last season since 2015-2016 (last time they made the playoffs), including an almost playoff birth with the Hornets making it to the play in. After the season, the Hornets decided to trade away some key pieces that made that play in appearance a reality. One of those players being Lamelo Ball, who has made an all-star appearance and was considered the face of the Hornets franchise. The hornets also traded away a couple other key pieces like Miles Bridges, Josh Green, Tre Mann, and some picks. The Hornets did gain some key players like Naz Reid, Grayson Allen, Royce O'Neale, Dorian Finney-Smith, and Dennis Schroder. Through my research, I am trying to figure out if the Hornets made the right decision for both short-term playoff potential through their 2026 offseason and long-term success or failure through trading Lamelo Ball. This question is relevant because the Hornets are making one of their biggest moves in an offseason in years to hopefully make it to the playoffs and doing that required many tuff decisions like trading away key players. Many people would care about these findings like basketball analysts, Hornets organization members, and hard-core Hornets fans wondering what our managers and coaching staff are deciding to do.
