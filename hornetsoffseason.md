Problem Definition 

Research Questions:

Context: The Charlotte Hornets had one of there best years this last season since 2015-2016 (last time they made the playoffs), including an almost playoff birth with the Hornets making it to the play in. After the season, the Hornets decided to trade away some key pieces that made that play in appearance a reality. One of those players being Lamelo Ball, who has made an all-star appreance and was considered the face of the Hornets franchise. The hornets also traded away a couple other key pieces like Miles Bridges, Josh Green, and some picks.
